Is InternShield really free?
Yes. Learning content, mentorship and the internship itself are completely free of cost. We do not charge any fee at any stage.
How long is the internship?
Programmes are typically structured around an 8-week duration, covering modules, hands-on tasks and a final project.
Which domains can I choose from?
Offensive Security, Defensive Security and Forensic Security. You can explore each domain's modules on the homepage before applying.
Do I get a certificate?
Yes — a Certificate of Internship, a Letter of Recommendation and a Project Completion Certificate are issued on successful completion.
Is prior experience required?
No prior cybersecurity experience is required — just genuine interest and willingness to learn and practice consistently.
How do I apply?
Use the "Apply Now" button to fill the internship interest form on the homepage. Our team will reach out with next steps.
