Privacy Policy

InternShield ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you apply for the internship programme, we collect your name, age, gender, phone number, email address and your selected learning domain/areas. This is submitted directly by you through our interest form.

How we use it

This information is used only to evaluate your application, communicate with you about the programme, and issue certificates/letters of recommendation upon successful completion. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

Data storage

This website currently operates without a backend server. Application details are transmitted directly to our team via email and a private notification channel (ntfy.sh) at the time of submission — no data is stored on the website itself.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising scripts.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your submitted information at any time by writing to info@internshield.in.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform grows. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Terms & Conditions

1. Free programme

InternShield's learning content and internship programme are completely free of cost. We do not charge any fee for enrolment, learning material, mentorship or certification.

2. Eligibility

Applicants should have a genuine interest in cybersecurity. There is no strict academic prerequisite, but a basic understanding of computers is helpful.

3. Code of conduct

All learning and practice must be carried out ethically and legally. Any misuse of skills taught (unauthorized hacking, data theft, etc.) is strictly against our values and may result in removal from the programme.

4. Project ownership

Projects built during the internship remain the intern's own work for portfolio purposes. InternShield may showcase anonymised project summaries for promotional use unless the intern opts out.

5. Certificates

Certificates, Letters of Recommendation and Project Completion certificates are issued only after successful completion of the assigned modules/tasks, at InternShield's discretion.

6. No placement guarantee

While we aim to build practical, job-relevant skills, InternShield does not guarantee employment or placement after completion of the internship.

7. Changes

These terms may be revised from time to time. Continued use of the programme implies acceptance of the latest terms.
